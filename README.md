# ONLYFRIDGE CLUB by [DevFridge](https://github.com/mikeminer/devfridge) — The Official Street Guide

> *"Open the fridge. Own the night."*

Welcome to **OnlyFridge Club**, a 3D multiplayer nightclub that lives in your browser. Palm trees outside, neon inside, a DJ who never sleeps and a giant fridge full of bottles behind the bar. You walk in, you meet people, you dance, you mix drinks, you bowl, you gamble with house chips, and — if your wallet has the right ice in it — you get past the bouncers of VELOUR.

This guide covers **everything you can do in the club**: every room, every game, every secret door and every command worth knowing.

> ⚠️ **House rules.** OnlyFridge Club runs in **practice mode**: tickets, chips and cosmetics have **no real value** and can't be bought or cashed out. The game never asks you to send tokens or sign transactions. Adults only (18+).

## 🚪 Getting in

1. **Pick your language** — 🇮🇹 Italiano, 🇺🇸 English or 🇵🇱 Polski. You can change it later from the flags on the entry screen or in the Menu.
2. **Build your character** — pick a name, woman or man, hairstyle and skin tone.
3. **Confirm you're 18+** and hit **Enter the club**.

You spawn at **The Threshold**, the foyer. The club is shared: everyone with the same invite link lands in the same **instance** (up to 40 real people). If it's full, you and your crew get moved together to the room next door.

> 💡 **Recommended browser:** an up-to-date Chrome or Edge on PC (WebGPU). Phones and tablets work too — the club switches to a lighter mode automatically.

The club is split into districts. Your current district is always shown top-left.

### 🎧 The Disk Joker

The DJ plays the **DevFridge Shorts** from YouTube, live and in sync for everyone in the club. When a new Short drops on the channel, it joins the set on its own. The small card top-left shows what's playing: hit **▶ Turn on the music**, and use **▾ / ▴** to shrink or enlarge the video. Walk into different rooms and the music changes with the space — full blast in The Core, muffled in the lounge.

### 📺 Screens worth reading

- **Tonight's program** board in the foyer.

- **Stage LED wall** — the club logo, now-playing and the vote countdown.

## 🕹️ The Neon Arcade

The biggest district. Everything here pays out **practice tickets** 🎟.

### 🎳 Bowling — two lanes, real physics

| | |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Walk to a lane, press **E** |
| **How** | Pick **position** → **direction** → **power** (click or Space each time). **Q / E** add left/right spin. The ball skids on the oil and hooks in the last metres |
| **Reward** | +5 🎟 per finished game |

**⚔️ Bowling duel (multiplayer)** — stand between the lanes and press **E** on *Bowling challenge*, then pick a nearby player. If they accept, you each get a lane and play **10 frames at the same time**. You can see the other lane live: their ball, their pins flying, their score on the lane monitor. Nobody can get more than one frame ahead. Highest score wins **+8 🎟**. Quit early and you lose by forfeit.

### 🎱 Pool — 8-ball vs the house AI

Aim with the mouse, set **power** and **spin**, shoot. **T** toggles the view. Simplified 8-ball rules; fouls give ball-in-hand. +4 🎟 to play, +6 🎟 if you beat the house.

### ⚽ Foosball — first to 5

Red vs blue. Move the pointer up and down to slide the rods, click (or Space) to shoot. +4 / +6 🎟.

### 🔫 Shooting gallery — "Tiro al Settimo"

60 seconds, cork pistol, **8 shots per magazine** (**R** to reload). Ducks, bottles, masks and **stars** (worth double — and they never stop). Hit in a row for a **combo multiplier** up to ×5. +3 🎟.

### 🔴 Quick Drop — the big red button

The ticket-redemption classic. A carousel of cups spins under a chute; every press of the **big red button** (Space, E, click or the on-screen **DROP**) releases one ball. Get **all 50 balls** into the cups in **60 seconds**.

- The carousel **speeds up** as you score and **reverses** at random. Watch it.
- A ball that clips a rim is a coin flip. Missed balls roll back to the end of the queue.
- The loader needs half a second between balls — mashing won't save you.
- **Rewards:** 1 🎟 every 5 balls, **+20 🎟 JACKPOT** for all 50.

### 🕹️ Hackathon Hall — real indie games

A row of arcade cabinets that load **real games** from the DevFridge hackathon (hackathon.devfridge.cool). Sit down, press **E**, play inside the club. Free demos run right there; full versions open in a new tab. The **catalogue terminal** lists everything. 60 seconds of play = +1 🎟.

> Empty cabinet? It says **YOUR GAME HERE** — press **E** to learn how to vibecode a game for your own pump.fun meme with the DevFridge Game Builder skill and get it into the arcade.

### 🏆 TopShelf — the prize showcase

A gold-framed glass case with the **season prize pool** of DevFridge World as spinning gold coins, plus a **leaderboard screen** next to it. Press **E** for the full prize list and ranking. (Display only — registration and claims happen on world.devfridge.cool.)

### 🍝 DevFridge World cabinet

A big cabinet for *The Italian Brainrot Game*. Before launch it shows a **countdown**; after launch it becomes **PLAY NOW** and opens the game inside the club.

### 🎰 Gacha machines & friends

| Spot | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Gacha — Masks / Style / Auras** | 3 🎟 per pull, cosmetics only |
| **Wardrobe** | Wear what you've won, change outfit, hair, skin |
| **Ticket desk** | Your balance, recent activity, how to earn more |

## 🍸 The BIG FRIDGE — cocktail bar

The back bar is a giant blue fridge: six frosted glass doors, lit shelves, bottles inside, cold mist rolling on the floor.

### Mixology stations 1–3

Walk to a station and press **E**. You build a drink in six steps:

1. **Glass** — highball, rocks, coupe, martini, flûte, copper mug.
2. **Ingredients** — spirits, liqueurs, aperitifs, wines, juices, syrups, mixers, bitters, zero-proof. **Hold to pour.**
3. **Technique** — built, shaken, stirred.
4. **Ice** — none, cubes, sphere, crushed.
5. **Garnish** — up to three.
6. **Name & serve** — the game rates the **balance** of your drink (too sweet? too sour?).

Save it to your **recipe book**, share it with a **link**, then drink it, **toast (T)** or **serve it (F)** to someone. First drink served each day: +2 🎟. Every drink made: +1 🎟.

### The other bar spots

- **Order from the menu** — the bartender makes a classic for you.
- **Recipe book** — every classic and every drink you've saved; guided "make it" mode and remixes.

> 🥤 Drinks are virtual and give no advantage. Zero-proof drinks count exactly the same.

## 💋 VELOUR — the private club

The club inside the club. Behind the door: stage dancers, private booths, lounge tables with table service, and the **casino**.

### Getting past the bouncers

**Marco** and **Dante** check you at the door. You need an **active DevFridge timelock of $PASTA or $TMC** in the wallet you show.

1. Talk to security → **Connect Phantom and verify**.
2. Phantom asks you to **sign a message**. That's all: it only proves the wallet is yours — **no transaction, no transfer, no spending**.
3. The server checks your timelocks on DevFridge and lets you in (or tells you why not).

> Don't have a timelock yet? Security gives you the official links to create one on DevFridge — and the fine print (lock period, 2% fee on claim) to read first.

### Inside

| Spot | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Stage** | Watch the night's line-up of dancers |
| **Private booths** | Sit down for a private dance; stand up whenever you like |
| **Lounge tables** | Order drinks to the table |
| **Roulette** | European, single zero |
| **Blackjack** | 6 decks, dealer stands on 17, blackjack pays 3:2, double on two cards |
| **Slot machines** | Published return ~95% |

**Casino chips are courtesy chips:** 1,000 free every day, no value, can't be bought or exchanged for anything.

## 🗳️ Cold Storage — the vote

**Every 8 minutes** the whole club votes which room opens next: **AURUM** (stage & runway), **CARATO** (the collectors' vault) or **PRISMA** (the hall of mirrors).

- A minute before, the DJ calls everyone to the dance floor.
- You have **25 seconds** to vote. One vote each, changeable until it closes.
- The winning room's curtain lifts and it **stays open until the next vote**.
- Taking part: +1 🎟.

What's inside:

- **AURUM** — walk the runway; all eyes on you.
- **CARATO** — your collection on display.
- **PRISMA** — mirrors everywhere: change your look.

## 🧊 The Seventh Door — secret room

Look for the black door marked **VII**. It slides open for a while **after every Cold Storage vote**.

Inside: **The Seventh Fridge**, a cold room with steel walls and a replica of the **DevFridge fridge**. Its LED shows the **live TVL** — the total USD value of every token timelocked in the DevFridge program. Press **E** to **open the door**: the shelves hold a jar for each locked token, with its logo and value. Press **E** again for the full list. There's also a **photo spot** in the corner.

### Chat

Press **Enter**. Tabs at the top: **All** (the whole instance), **Nearby** (12 m around you), **Room** (your district), **Private**. Mention someone with **@name** and they get a ping. Click a name for **Whisper / Mention / Block / Report**.

> 🛡️ **Block** hides that person's chat, drinks and avatar for you. **Report** sends their last messages to the host and also blocks them.

## 💡 Pro tips

- **Quick Drop:** don't watch the chute — watch the cup **coming**. Press a beat *before* it arrives, and expect the reverse.
- **Bowling:** throw from the right edge with a little ⟲ spin; the hook does the rest in the last metres.
- **Cold Storage:** be on the dance floor when the DJ calls — you only get 25 seconds.
- **The Seventh Door** stays open for a while after the vote. Don't dawdle.
- **Laggy?** Menu → *Quality: Low*. On weaker devices the club also lowers resolution by itself when frames drop.
- **Frozen picture after a graphics hiccup?** Reload the page — your tickets, looks and recipes are saved on your device.

## 🛠️ Running your own club (hosts)

Want to host OnlyFridge Club on your PC?

1. Run **`OnlyFridgeClub-Setup-1.0.0.exe`** — no admin rights, Node.js and cloudflared included.
2. Open **OnlyFridge Club** from the desktop: that's the **control panel**.

| Panel key | Does |
|---|---|
| **1 / 2 / 3** | Start / stop / restart the server |
| **4 / 5** | Stats / **live stats**: players online and where they are, traffic, bowling duels, TVL, TopShelf, sponsors, DJ set, reports |
| **6 / 7** | Server log / graphics errors reported by players |
| **0** | Play: start if needed and open the club |
| **T / C / L** | Open / close / log of a **temporary Cloudflare bridge** — a public `https://…trycloudflare.com` link for friends outside your network (copied to your clipboard) |

The club runs on `http://localhost:8080`. Anyone with your bridge link can walk in — share it only with people you want inside.
